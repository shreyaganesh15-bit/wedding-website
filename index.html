One very full, very lovely day

A Tamil Brahmin wedding is a morning of rituals, music, family and a little happy chaos. Here’s a glimpse of what’s ahead.

(No quiz afterwards, promise)

  1. first light

    The morning begins

    Family gathers, lamps are lit, and the celebrations begin.

  2. a small detour

    Kashi Yatra

    A playful tradition involving an umbrella, a walking stick and a groom who briefly considers another path.

  3. a playful exchange

    Garlands & Oonjal

    Garlands are exchanged, the swing comes out, and the singing begins.

  4. Between 9:00 & 10:00 AM

    9:00 to 10:00 AM

    The Muhurtham

    The heart of the ceremony: the thaali is tied to the sound of the getti melam.

  5. seven steps together

    Saptapadi

    Seven steps around the sacred fire, followed by blessings from family.

  6. come hungry

    Lunch

    A traditional South Indian meal on a banana leaf. Tip: eat with your hands.
